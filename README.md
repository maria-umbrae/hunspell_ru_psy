**Russian Psychiatric Hunspell Dictionary**

This dictionary is intended to complement general-purpose Russian dictionaries. It focuses on terms that may be absent from standard dictionaries or may be useful specifically in psychiatric and clinical texts, including diagnostic terminology, symptoms, syndromes, medications, eponyms, abbreviations, and related medical terms.

It is not intended to replace a general Russian dictionary. For the best results, use it together with a standard Russian [Hunspell](https://github.com/hunspell/hunspell) dictionary.
